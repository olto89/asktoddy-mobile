# AskToddy Mobile - Scraping Targets Analysis

## 🎯 Priority Scraping Targets for Key Categories

### 1. **ELECTRICAL & PLUMBING** 🔌🚿

#### ✅ **LUCKINSlive** - EXCELLENT DISCOVERY

- **What**: UK's most comprehensive online costs directory
- **Coverage**: 1 million+ items from hundreds of electrical, HVAC, plumbing manufacturers
- **Access**: FREE directly on site
- **Scraping Feasibility**: HIGH - appears to be open access
- **Value**: MASSIVE - single source for trade pricing

#### Electrical Specialists

- **CEF (City Electrical Factors)**: 40,000+ products, 400 stores
  - Likely has login requirements for trade pricing
  - Scraping: MEDIUM difficulty
- **Edmundson Electrical**: Major wholesaler, on LUCKINSlive
  - Better to scrape via LUCKINSlive
- **Yesss Electrical**: Regional alternative
  - Scraping: MEDIUM-HIGH feasibility

#### Plumbing Specialists

- **City Plumbing Supplies**: Part of Buying Group, exclusive contract prices
  - Likely requires trade account
  - Scraping: LOW feasibility
- **Graham (Ray Grahams)**: DIY store with online pricing
  - Scraping: HIGH feasibility (smaller retailer)
- **PlumbNation/PlumbWorld**: Online specialists
  - Scraping: MEDIUM-HIGH feasibility
