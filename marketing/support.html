Frequently asked questions

How do AskToddy quotes work?

You capture a short site assessment — job type, measurements, notes and photos — and AskToddy's AI generates an itemised quote with realistic UK labour and material costs. You can edit any line, add your margin, and export a branded PDF to send to your client.

Are the quotes accurate?

Quotes are indicative estimates to help you plan and quote faster — not guaranteed prices. Always confirm final figures against your own costs and supplier prices before sending a formal quote. See our Terms & Conditions for the full disclaimer.

What's included in the free plan?

The free plan includes 5 AI-generated quotes per month, the full site assessment flow, PDF export, and saved quotes. Upgrade to Pro (£9.99/month) for unlimited quotes.

How do I manage or cancel my subscription?

Subscriptions are billed through your Apple App Store account. To manage or cancel, open the Settings app on your iPhone, tap your name, then Subscriptions, and select AskToddy. Cancelling stops future billing; you keep Pro access until the end of the current period.

How do I delete my account?

You can delete your account and all associated data from within the app, under Account settings. Alternatively, email support@asktoddy.co.uk and we'll remove your data within 30 days. See our Privacy Policy for details.

I didn't receive my confirmation email

Check your spam or junk folder. If it still hasn't arrived after a few minutes, request a new one from the sign-in screen, or contact support@asktoddy.co.uk.
