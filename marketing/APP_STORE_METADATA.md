## Description (max 4000 chars)

AskToddy is the fastest way for UK tradespeople and homeowners to turn a site assessment into a professional, itemised construction quote — powered by AI.

Stop losing evenings to paperwork. Capture the job on-site in a few taps, and let AskToddy generate a detailed cost breakdown with realistic UK labour and material prices. Tweak the figures, add your margin, and send a clean, branded PDF before you've left the driveway.

WHAT YOU CAN DO
• Guided site assessment — capture job type, measurements, notes and photos with no blank page to stare at.
• AI-priced quotes — get an itemised breakdown across nine job types: extensions, kitchens, bathrooms, patios, driveways, conservatories, roofing, renovations and more.
• Edit every line — adjust prices and add your margin, with totals that recalculate instantly.
• Branded PDF export — add your company details and logo, then share a professional quote straight from your phone.
• Works offline — capture notes without signal and generate the quote once you're back online.
• Saved quotes — every quote is stored securely to your account, ready to revisit and reuse.

HOW IT WORKS

1. Create a free account.
2. Capture a quick site assessment.
3. Let AskToddy's AI build your itemised quote.
4. Edit, brand, and share as a PDF.

FREE & PRO
AskToddy is free to use with 5 AI-generated quotes per month. Upgrade to Pro for £9.99/month to unlock unlimited quotes. Cancel anytime.

Please note: AskToddy quotes are indicative estimates to help you plan and quote faster — not guaranteed prices. Always confirm final figures against your own costs before sending a formal quote.

Questions? Email support@asktoddy.co.uk
